My story

Food, health and freedom.

For years, I've been fascinated by the relationship between the food we eat and the way it affects our bodies. I've spent countless hours researching how to eat well, improve health and lose weight in a way that is both effective and sustainable.

I became especially interested in the role insulin plays in the body, and how it can stand in the way of weight loss. A low-carb, high-fat way of eating — I prefer not to call it a diet — combined with proper meal timing is one of the most effective strategies I've come across.

It's personal, too. After years of struggling with my own relationship with food and certain health issues, I'm thankful to have overcome many of those obstacles. The freedom that comes with that is something I want every woman to experience.

That's why I started The Second Half Project: for women who feel they've failed over and over again, and who long for a healthier body and, ultimately, a healthier life.

Why "The Second Half"?

The choices you make today will shape the Second Half of your life.

That's the meaning behind the name. The habits you build now decide how you'll feel in the years ahead.

And it's a project for a reason: work you do on purpose, step by step, with a plan. Not another quick fix.

What I believe

Firm with the habits. Gentle with you.

Normal is not the same as healthy.

Stubborn weight, low energy and poor sleep have become so common that they feel normal. They don't have to be your normal.

Willpower isn't the answer.

If you're constantly hungry, you'll eventually lose the battle. The answer isn't to fight harder, but to change the way your body responds, so cravings settle and hunger is under control.

Gradual change. Lasting results.

Small, steady habits, one step at a time. No crash diets, no punishing workouts, no starting over.

I believe God designed our bodies to function the way they were made to, when we steward and nourish them well.
