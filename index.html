The 12-week 1:1 coaching program

How it works.

Real food, one new habit a week, and someone by your side. I won't promise you a number on the scale, but I firmly believe this way of eating and living gives your body the best possible chance to lose weight naturally, and keep it off.

01

A fresh start in your kitchen

We begin with an intake consultation of about an hour, to understand your history and your routine. Your starter hamper arrives at your door, and in your first week we reset your kitchen, so it works for you, not against you.

02

One new habit a week

A 30-minute consultation every week, with a new guide and one new habit that builds on the last. You eat the Second Half way: real food, low in sugar and starch.

03

Someone by your side

A daily food log that I read, and WhatsApp support (Mon–Fri 09:00–16:00, Sat 08:00–10:00) for questions, plate photos and the days it feels hard.

No exercise required. Movement is good for you — it's just not the starting point.
