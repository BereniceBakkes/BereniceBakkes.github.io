The 12-week 1:1 coaching program

A plan. A person. For good.

01

Start with a conversation

An intake consultation of about an hour, to understand your history, your routine and what hasn't worked before. Your starter hamper arrives at your door.

02

One step a week

A 30-minute consultation every week, with a new guide and clear goals. Small, steady habits instead of a lifestyle overhaul.

03

Someone beside you

Daily WhatsApp support (Mon–Fri, 08:00–17:00) for questions, meal photos and the days it feels hard.

No exercise required. Movement is good for you — it's just not the starting point.
