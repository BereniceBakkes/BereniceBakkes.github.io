Is this for you?

Made for women who are tired of starting over.

This is for you if…

  • You've tried calorie counting, keto or other diets, and the weight came back.
  • You don't know what to eat, when to eat or where to start.
  • You do better with structure and someone keeping you on track.
  • You're ready to change your daily habits, one step at a time.
  • You'd like results without having to start exercising.

It's not for you if…

  • You're looking for a quick fix or a crash diet.
  • You want a workout program.
  • You're not ready to change what's on your plate.
  • You have a medical condition or take medication that needs close medical supervision. Please speak to your doctor first.

A question I hear often

Do I have to give up carbs and sugar forever?

You lose the weight on a strict keto approach. Then you learn to live more flexibly, but still healthily. You won't go back to sugar and the "normal" way of eating, but you'll know how to have something special without undoing your progress.
